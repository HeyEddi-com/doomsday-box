## Risks & challenges (outline for KS)

- OEM lead times and LattePanda / chassis availability  
- Landed BOM and fee variance vs research sheet in `campaign-economics.md`  
- Software beta quality before public OSS cut  
- Home network topologies (AP vs inline bridge) vary by household  
- Shipping delays outside our control  
- Dual-path Ethernet QC (dongle + cables)  
- Open options (cooling, base storage) changing margins
