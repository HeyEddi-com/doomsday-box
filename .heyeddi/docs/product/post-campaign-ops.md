# Post-campaign operations

**Last updated:** 2026-07-23  
**Status:** Locked defaults  
**Guidance:** `.docs/post kickstarter.md`

## Goals

1. Fulfill rewards  
2. Split retained net between operating runway and next inventory (planning default)  
3. Retail without a second simultaneous crowdfunding campaign  

## Fulfillment

| Stream | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Digital Insiders | Discord invite + build access + CONTRIBUTORS credit |
| Hardware | OEM → Zapopan QA → pack → ship → KS tracking updates |
| OSS | Public `heyeddi/doomsday-box` v1.0 at ship (`oss-release-promise.md`) |

**Fulfillment model:** **Self-ship from Zapopan** until sustained **>50 units/month**, then evaluate 3PL.
